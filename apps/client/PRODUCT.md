# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: individuals in Africa exploring homeownership and urban living, moving from aspiration toward an informed decision — evaluating options, building readiness (HomeReady™), and identifying an appropriate next step.

Secondary: organisations (developers, employers, NGOs, financial institutions) considering a partnership with AXP to expand access to accessible homeownership, reached via Work with AXP.

## Product Purpose

AXP Africa helps individuals and organisations unlock homeownership and urban-living opportunities through trusted guidance, thoughtful preparation and clearer next steps. Success is a visitor leaving with clearer information, stronger preparation and an appropriate next step — not necessarily a completed transaction.

## Positioning

"AXP is not selling houses. AXP is helping people own homes." AXP differentiates from real-estate listing/brokerage sites by pairing responsibly presented opportunities with a structured readiness program (HomeReady™) and straightforward guidance, keeping visitor confidence and clarity central rather than transaction volume or listing count.

## Operating Context

Public marketing/informational site (React SPA) with light website accounts (email + password, held in the AXP CRM). Enquiries, interest forms and sign-ups all land in the AXP CRM. Content areas: home, home-ownership-opportunities (+ opportunity-detail, which captures visitor interest via a form rather than a purchase/checkout flow), homeready (the HomeReady™ readiness program), impact, knowledge, who-we-are, work-with-axp (partner outreach), careers, media, contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Opportunity detail pages capture interest via a lead-capture form (InterestForm); there is no transactional/checkout flow.
- Business model is still forming — do not assume or imply commission, referral fees, or a specific monetization mechanic in design or copy.
- Accounts exist (sign-up, sign-in, a dashboard) but are **not yet wired to a specific product feature** — the dashboard shows example previews only. No confirmed save-progress, saved-opportunities or partner-portal capability yet. Treat any such capability as undecided, not implemented.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: AXP Africa. Tagline: "Making Urban Living Possible."
- Logo assets: `/images/axp_logo_reversed.png`, `/images/axp_logo_cropped.png`.
- Established palette: deep navy (`#1A233D`, see `theme-color`) as the primary/dark surface color, with a gold accent (`eyebrow--gold`, `button--gold`).
- Voice: measured, trust-first, non-salesy — e.g. "AXP is not selling houses. AXP is helping people own homes."

## Evidence on Hand

Real, cited content: Africa housing-challenge statistics on the home page (70m estimated housing-unit deficit — UN-Habitat, 2025; 900m+ additional urban residents by 2050 — UN-Habitat World Cities Report 2026; <5% mortgage penetration as a share of GDP — CAHF Yearbook 2024), plus the vision/mission/promise copy on Who We Are.

Illustrative/placeholder: specific opportunity listings (the property/program details under Home Ownership Opportunities and Opportunity Detail) stand in for future real listings. Do not treat specific listing details as confirmed product fact, and do not extend or elaborate them as if real.

## Product Principles

- Guidance over selling: every surface should read as helping people decide well, not pushing a transaction.
- Clarity and responsible presentation: opportunities are "clearly labelled," not oversold; avoid inflated or unverifiable claims.
- Confidence at the centre: visitor trust and understanding are the success metric, ahead of raw conversion volume.
- Dual audience: individual home-seekers and partner organisations are both first-class visitors with distinct journeys (explore/prepare vs. Work with AXP).
- Evidence-based urgency: motivate with cited, sourced data (UN-Habitat, CAHF) — never fabricate stats or testimonials.
